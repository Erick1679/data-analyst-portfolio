Business Objective

The objective of this project was to develop an enterprise-style analytics platform capable of supporting strategic decisions across customer retention, revenue growth, customer value management, product performance, and operational efficiency.

The platform combines data preparation, predictive modelling, interactive dashboards, and executive reporting into one complete analytics solution.

Dashboard Intelligence Modules

The platform contains eight interactive analytics modules, each designed to answer specific business questions.

Business Intelligence Coverage

  • Executive Overview: High-level KPIs, revenue performance, customer metrics, and business summaries.
  • Revenue Forecasting: Historical sales analysis and future revenue prediction.
  • Customer Intelligence: Customer behaviour analysis, segmentation, and retention insights.
  • Customer Lifetime Value: Customer value estimation and segment analysis.
  • Product Analytics: Product performance and sales contribution analysis.
  • Operations & Inventory: Stock visibility and operational monitoring.
  • Model Performance: Machine learning evaluation and predictive accuracy.
  • Data Quality: Dataset validation and quality reporting.
